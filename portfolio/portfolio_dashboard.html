Formal Guarantees (VERIFY block)

The VERIFY block provides mathematically proven guarantees about your portfolio strategy. It is not a prediction. It is a proof. The compiler guarantees that under the fitted market model, the recommended strategy CANNOT recommend aggressive equity allocation when the posterior belief in a bear/crisis regime exceeds the threshold encoded in the rewards. This is a formal safety property, verified at compile time, not learned from backtesting.
